## Safety

The AI must not present itself as a licensed clinician or make autonomous clinical diagnoses.

Clinical or therapeutic content must be reviewed by qualified professionals before clinical use.

Real patient/session data must not be used for model training without appropriate consent, legal basis, de-identification and governance.

API keys and secrets must remain server-side and must never be embedded in the mobile application.

The 35 Silwan principles are treated as a product/content framework. Unverified spiritual, historical or medical claims must not be presented as established medical facts.
